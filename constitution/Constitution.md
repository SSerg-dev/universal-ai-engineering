## 1. Purpose

This Constitution defines the highest governing authority for the Universal AI Engineering ecosystem.

## 2. Mission

Universal AI Engineering exists to help humans and AI agents build AI-first systems with explicit accountability, traceable decisions, and evidence-based verification.

## 3. Governing Motto

Architecture first. Specifications second. Implementation third. Evidence always.

## 4. Scope of Constitutional Authority

This Constitution governs every architecture document, standard, methodology, template, reference asset, implementation, and educational artifact in the ecosystem.

## 5. Constitutional Rule #0

No implementation may redefine the governing architecture.

If an implementation, methodology, template, book, or reference asset conflicts with the governing architecture, the lower-level artifact MUST be corrected unless the governing architecture is formally amended.

## 6. Architectural Authority

Architecture defines the placement, responsibility, and allowed dependencies of ecosystem artifacts. Implementation artifacts MUST follow the approved architecture.

## 7. Hierarchical Authority

Lower-level artifacts MUST NOT override or contradict higher-level artifacts.

## 8. Single Source of Truth

Normative rules SHOULD be defined once in the highest applicable authoritative artifact and referenced elsewhere.

## 9. Artifact Traceability

Governed artifacts MUST identify their stable ID, type, version, status, parent, dependencies, and repository location.

## 10. Human Accountability

AI agents MAY assist with analysis, planning, implementation, review, or documentation, but accountable human approval remains required for governance decisions and merges.

## 11. Evidence-Based Verification

Claims of completion, conformance, or readiness MUST be supported by reviewable evidence.

## 12. Vendor Independence

Universal AI Engineering MUST remain independent of any mandatory model, tool, IDE, cloud provider, or agent vendor.

## 13. Controlled Evolution

Material changes to governing artifacts require review, traceability, and human approval.

## 14. Amendment Process

Constitutional amendments require a proposal, impact analysis, architectural review, recorded decision, version update, and explicit human approval.

## 15. Ratification Status

This Constitution is Draft. It is not approved or stable until explicitly ratified by the accountable human authority.
